# Issue I: State-preservation evidence boundary

## Risk level

P1 / Medium.

## Potential reviewer concern

CCD intensity images show expected transverse profiles, but do not directly prove full complex amplitude/phase preservation or single-photon state preservation.

## Why this matters

The paper's motivation includes state-preserving OAM sorting. The experimental evidence should be described at the right level: profile preservation observed in intensity, mechanism-based preservation inferred from the linear FP transfer function, not full field tomography.

## Current evidence

- CCD images show expected OAM-dependent transverse intensity structures at resonance.
- FP cavity transmission is a linear spectral selection process for cavity eigenmodes.
- The current experiment does not include phase reconstruction or interferometric verification of the full complex field.

## Can claim

- The transmitted intensity profiles are consistent with the expected target modes.
- The FP cavity does not intentionally perform a transverse coordinate transformation; it spectrally selects resonant transverse eigenmodes.
- The experiment supports transverse-profile preservation in the measured intensity sense.

## Can cautiously say

- Full complex-field preservation is supported by the FP cavity's linear eigenmode response and prior FP-based OAM sorting literature, but not directly measured here by phase tomography.

## Should not claim

- Do not claim the CCD images alone prove complete complex amplitude and phase preservation.
- Do not claim single-photon quantum state preservation from classical intensity images alone.

## Safe response wording

The CCD images demonstrate preservation of the transverse intensity profiles at the resonance peaks. We agree that these images alone do not constitute a full complex-field or single-photon state tomography. The state-preserving character referred to here follows from the linear FP-cavity eigenmode response, while the present experiment directly verifies transverse-profile consistency in the measured intensity.

## Optional supplement/table/figure

- Clarifying sentence distinguishing intensity-profile preservation from full complex-field tomography.
- Optional future-work sentence about interferometric phase verification if requested.

## If reviewer pushes harder

Offer to soften wording in abstract/conclusion. If they require direct evidence, acknowledge that additional phase-sensitive or interferometric measurement would be needed and frame it as beyond the current scope.

## Action if reviewer explicitly asks

Revise text claims; no new data unless the editor/reviewer specifically requires phase evidence.
